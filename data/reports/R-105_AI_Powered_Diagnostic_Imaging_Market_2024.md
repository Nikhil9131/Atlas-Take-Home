## Clinical Diagnostics Context
Healthcare systems face acute radiologist shortages and surging diagnostic scan volumes. Computer-assisted detection (CADe) and diagnostic triage software powered by deep learning convolutional neural networks are transforming radiological workflows in MRI, CT, and digital mammography.

## Market Valuation & Clinical Adoption Projections
The global market for AI-powered diagnostic imaging was valued at USD 2.45 billion in 2024. With regulatory agencies approving over 120 new clinical algorithms in 2024 alone, the market is projected to expand at an extraordinary CAGR of 24.1% over the 2024–2029 forecast period.
