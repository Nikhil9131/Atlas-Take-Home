## Market Overview & Scope
Growing awareness regarding municipal tap water contamination, industrial microplastics, and groundwater heavy-metal concentrations has propelled consumer investment into household point-of-use (POU) water purification systems. This report provides strategic insights across residential point-of-use reverse osmosis (RO) systems, ultraviolet (UV) disinfection purifiers, and multi-stage gravity-based filtration units.

## Global Market Sizing and Growth Horizons
In 2024, the global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 9.55 billion. Propelled by accelerating urbanization and expanding middle-class disposable incomes across emerging economies, the market is projected to expand at a CAGR of 7.8% from 2025 to 2030.

### Technology Segmentation
* **Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems:** Represent the largest revenue segment (58% share in 2024), essential in regions with high Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) exceeding 500 ppm.
* **Ultraviolet (UV) Systems:** Capture 26% of market value, favored in municipal zones where water has low mineralization but biological pathogen contamination risks.
* **Gravity and Activated Carbon:** Account for the remaining 16%, primarily serving entry-level and off-grid consumers.

## Regional Dynamics & Geographic Concentration
The Asia-Pacific (APAC) region dominates the global residential water purifier industry, commanding a 52% share of global revenue in 2024. Rapid urbanization, increasing health awareness, and widespread public water quality variability in Tier-2 and Tier-3 urban centers across China, India, Indonesia, and Vietnam continue to catalyze multi-year double-digit consumer adoption.

North America and Europe follow with combined revenue share of 36%, primarily concentrated in advanced multi-stage under-sink remineralization filters and smart IoT-connected cartridge replacement indicators.
