## Executive Summary
Commercial fleet operators across logistics, municipal transit, and delivery services are transitioning from internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles to medium- and heavy-duty battery electric fleets. This report assesses global expenditures on high-power depot charging hardware, grid interconnect systems, and intelligent charge management software.

## Market Sizing and Growth Forecast
The global commercial EV fleet charging infrastructure market was valued at USD 5.10 billion in 2024. As fleet operators build out dedicated megawatt-scale depot charging hubs, the market is forecast to grow at a CAGR of 18.5% from 2025 to 2030.

### Infrastructure Categories
* **DC Fast Chargers (150kW - 350kW):** Account for 62% of depot equipment spending.
* **Depot Energy Management Software:** SaaS telemetry, dynamic load shedding, and time-of-use tariff optimization platforms represent 22% of market value.
* **Grid Interconnection Substation Upgrades:** Balance of spending at 16%.
