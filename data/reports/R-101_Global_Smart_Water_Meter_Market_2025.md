## Technological Evolution & Architecture
Smart water meter deployments rely on two predominant architectures:
- **AMR (Automated Meter Reading):** One-way drive-by or walk-by radio-frequency data transmission that reduces manual meter-reading labor but offers limited bidirectional capability.
- **AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure):** Continuous, two-way fixed-base cellular (NB-IoT, LTE-M) and LPWAN (LoRaWAN, Sigfox) mesh networks transmitting hourly consumption readings, pressure profiles, and reverse-flow leak alerts directly to utility operational centers.

Utilities in mature geographies are transitioning from legacy mechanical meters to solid-state ultrasonic meters that feature no moving mechanical parts, ensuring sustained measurement accuracy over a 15- to 20-year operational lifecycle.

## Regulatory Landscape & Municipal Mandates
Municipal utility regulations increasingly incentivize non-revenue water mitigation. In developing metropolitan areas, unbilled water consumption due to physical pipeline leakage and unauthorized extraction exceeds 35% of total treated volume. Ultrasonic AMI infrastructure enables immediate acoustic leak triangulation, reducing loss ratios by up to 60% within 18 months of deployment.
