## Enterprise Security Perimeter Shift
As multi-cloud adoption accelerates, traditional perimeter firewalls fail to secure unstructured data residing across AWS S3 buckets, Google Cloud Storage, and Snowflake data warehouses. Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) and cloud access security brokers (CASB) are central pillars in contemporary CISO strategies.

## Market Sizing & Enterprise Spending Forecast
The global cloud-native data security market attained a valuation of USD 3.60 billion in 2024. Fueled by stringent data sovereignty compliance laws (GDPR, CCPA, Digital India DPDP Act) and generative AI data privacy exposure concerns, the market is forecasted to grow at a CAGR of 16.2% between 2025 and 2030.

### Segment Breakdown
* **Data Security Posture Management (DSPM):** 44% share of new enterprise licensing.
* **Cloud Access Security Broker (CASB) & Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA):** 36% market share.
* **Tokenization & Confidential Computing Hardware Security Modules:** 20% share.
