## Hardware Technology Trends: Mechanical vs Solid-State
Mechanical volumetric and velocity meters continue to represent a significant installed base in cost-sensitive markets. However, solid-state static meters (primarily transit-time ultrasonic sensors) represent the overwhelming majority of new capital allocations among investor-owned utilities in urban centers. Static meters maintain calibration stability across low-flow residential regimes where mechanical meters suffer mechanical slip and register under-recording.

## Procurement Recommendations for Water Authorities
Utility engineering directors are advised to require open protocol standards (such as DLMS/COSEM or OMS) across all purchased smart water meter hardware to avoid single-vendor lock-in during multi-phase decade-long municipal rollouts.
